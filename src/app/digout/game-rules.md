#Digout Rules

- rules are the same as traditional minesweeper game, with a few changes
- first, you are not given numbers indicating adjacent mines. instead you are given shapes
- the shapes correspond to numbers, and the numbers they correspond to change every day
- its up to you, the player, to infer what numbers they represent by playing the game
- you have 3 'lives' to correctly infer what the numbers are, otherwise you lose
